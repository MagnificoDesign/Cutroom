## Current behavior and limits

- The interpolation path uses our local, bounded pyramidal Lucas–Kanade tracker, reverse-track validation, forward/backward warps and a cubic timing curve. No RIFE/FILM/Framegen model, new dependency, CDN, paid API or remote compute is bundled. Neural interpolation remains a separate future evaluation.
- The planner compares up to 16 coarse candidates per side plus bounded fine/pause candidates. It scores the actual outgoing frame, separates coherent local movement from camera motion, and predicts the next picture. The application uses a cooperative search that yields between clip pairs and beam depths.
- An eligible ordinary join can replace 2–8 existing video slots around the seam. Original edit duration, audio sample count, source audio and pitch remain unchanged; sound uses the same existing 5 ms edge ramps. This does not invent or extend a missing audio passage.
- Motion must advance consistently on both sides. Large gaps, opposite direction, weak/ambiguous tracks, new local detail, flashes and incompatible aspect ratios receive clean cuts. Brief interpolation is evaluated using both small analysis images and output-resolution color samples. These checks reduce artifacts but are not a semantic correctness guarantee for arbitrary footage.
- Verified overlaps and all clips involved in uncertain overlap candidates bypass interpolation. The full-clips action also disables it. No source frames are changed in the vault. Optional Review edits reports the generated frame count and affected joins.
- Preparation reads source movies sequentially rather than retaining two decrypted source Blobs. A bridge holds only its two full-resolution endpoint images and small motion maps. The renderer may retain an incoming and outgoing bridge at once for a middle clip, and releases each after use. Pixel generation yields every 16 rows; all stages check cancellation.
- Every selected clip contributes to the edit. Uncertain overlap candidates and contained/full duplicates are kept. Verified shared picture and source sound are used once.
- Ordinary new trims must satisfy the existing visual/motion criteria plus quiet sound for at least 80 ms on each side of the cut. Pause candidates require a longer quiet span. This detects acoustic gaps, not words, sentence endings, highlights or story meaning.
- Silent clips remain eligible for visual cuts. Continuous audible clips, failed sound inspection and uninspected times retain their original boundaries. Original file boundaries and rigorously verified overlap joins remain available.
- Acoustic inspection covers whole clips up to 24 seconds. Longer clips use at most 24 seconds in merged windows near clip ends and the initial visual cut choices. Extra pause images and the subsequent joint search remain bounded.
- Up to 12 selected clips; finished output up to three minutes, roughly 30 fps and a longest edge of 1280 pixels. Overlap verification remains bounded to 0.8–12 seconds, ordinary rates up to 60 fps and at most 24 candidate pairs.
- Up to 12 queue thumbnails are made sequentially. Images and the one open source preview are transient; lock/cancellation prevents stale media returning. No plaintext thumbnail, source copy or finished export is persisted in IndexedDB.
- Remove changes the encrypted current selection. Undo restores only the most recent removal and is temporary: its encrypted copy is discarded on the next import, creation, removal or lock, and reopening cleans it if background work was suspended. No Previous imports archive is kept. Original Photos/Files videos are unchanged.
- Edit These Clips keeps the queue and releases the prior result. Create New Video deletes all imported metadata/chunks and clears the queue without requiring a save, including a pending Undo or failed-file reference. Deletion and the empty encrypted selection commit in one transaction under the existing cross-tab write lock; failed/cancelled cleanup rolls back. Join previews use the renderer's sample-precision output timeline, not unrounded source cut sums.
- Browser storage remains subject to eviction/clearing. No absolute privacy guarantee, Face ID implementation or independent security audit is claimed.
